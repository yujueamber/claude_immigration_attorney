# NIW Narrative Review

Use this skill as a **strict quality gate** for EB-2 NIW proposed endeavors, evidence maps, petition narratives, and RFE-risk reviews.

## Required Reading

Before reviewing an NIW narrative, read:

1. `knowledge/overview-niw.md`
2. all files in `knowledge/prongs/`
3. `knowledge/evidence-hierarchy.md`
4. `knowledge/argument-patterns.md`
5. `knowledge/uscis-policy-alerts.md`
6. `knowledge/niw-narrative-review-guidelines.md`
7. the matter's `00_CASE_DASHBOARD.md`
8. every completed deliverable referenced by the dashboard that is relevant to the review

## Review Workflow

1. Identify the exact proposed endeavor.
2. Run the **Employer Removal Test**.
3. Run the **Generic Engineer Test**.
4. Separate all important statements into:
   - Established Fact
   - Supported Analysis
   - Future Plan
   - Unsupported/Speculative
5. Verify every major claim against the evidence/claim registry.
6. Flag employer benefit presented as national importance.
7. Flag team outcomes presented as personal outcomes.
8. Flag future GA/open-source/adoption as completed.
9. Audit each Dhanasar prong separately.
10. Produce a skeptical-officer review before accepting the narrative.

## Required Output

### 1. 30-Second Case Logic
State:
- problem,
- method,
- national-importance theory,
- why the applicant is well positioned,
- why the balancing favors a waiver.

### 2. Proposed Endeavor Assessment
State whether the endeavor is:
- specific,
- technically coherent,
- broader than one employer,
- realistically executable.

### 3. Prong 1 Assessment
Evaluate:
- substantial merit,
- specific national-importance theory,
- independent evidence,
- broader-impact mechanism,
- speculative gaps.

### 4. Prong 2 Assessment
Evaluate:
- individual contribution,
- verified outcomes,
- anchor-project relevance,
- execution track record,
- corroborating evidence.

### 5. Prong 3 Assessment
Evaluate:
- independent balancing logic,
- relationship between endeavor and permanent job,
- actual dissemination/collaboration path,
- whether the argument merely repeats Prong 1.

### 6. Claim-to-Evidence Map
For each major claim, identify supporting Evidence IDs.

### 7. Top 3 RFE Risks
State the strongest skeptical-officer objections.

### 8. Required Revisions
Give concrete changes, not generic advice.

### 9. Readiness
Use exactly one:
- **Ready for attorney review**
- **Promising, but material gaps remain**
- **Reframe before filing**

Do not assign an approval probability without a defensible empirical basis.

## Hard Rules

- Never fabricate evidence, metrics, adoption, novelty, or legal standards.
- Never convert a possible future effect into an achieved outcome.
- Never treat employer scale as proof of national importance.
- Never treat a customer count as proof of the applicant's individual impact without attribution evidence.
- Never hide a material evidence gap with stronger wording.
- Never draft a final petition merely because the prose sounds persuasive.
